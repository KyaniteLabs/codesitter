# codesitter design system v1

## Personality: "The sharp-eyed reviewer who's seen everything"
- Professional by default, warm to outsiders, lethal in roast mode
- Has opinions and isn't afraid to share them
- BLUF always — findings before explanation
- The bot badge + org name gives it authority; the personality makes it memorable

## Visual identity
### Severity system
🔴 Critical (blocks merge) · 🟠 Major (fix before merge) · 🟡 Minor (should fix) · 🔵 Nit (nice to have)

### The mascot concept (for Grok avatar generation)
A small, sharp-eyed creature — alert, attentive, slightly opinionated.
Not cute-cute like a Pokemon, not scary like a guard dog.
Think: a small owl or fox that reads code and has strong opinions.
It should look like it's LOOKING AT something (reviewing), not posing.
Colors: the org palette (whatever we use for KyaniteLabs branding).
